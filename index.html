GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

Before we
get started.

How much will my website cost?

Tell me what you need and I’ll prepare a tailored quote. The price depends on the number of pages, design requirements, content, and integrations. No package price is assumed before we agree on scope.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. Share your current website and what you want to improve. We can discuss a visual refresh, clearer content, better mobile layouts, or a more substantial rebuild.

Do I need my content ready?

A logo, photos, and a rough description of your services are a useful starting point. We’ll agree on what you supply and whether help organizing or preparing content is part of the project.

Are these real client websites?

The six business showcases are original design concepts for fictional brands. They demonstrate possible visual directions; they are not testimonials or completed client commissions.

What about hosting, updates, and online payments?

We’ll choose the right setup for your requirements. Hosting, domain ownership, payment providers, ongoing changes, and any recurring costs are discussed explicitly in the project scope.
